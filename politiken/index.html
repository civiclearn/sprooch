Terms of Use

Last updated: February 2026

Service description

Sproochentest.org provides online preparation material for the Sproochentest, the Luxembourgish language exam required for Luxembourg nationality. The platform includes test simulations, listening exercises, speaking practice, and learning resources.

Not an official exam

Sproochentest.org is an independent preparation platform. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the Institut national des langues Luxembourg (INLL) or any Luxembourg government body. Our simulations do not lead to any official certification. Registration for the official Sproochentest must be done directly via MyINL.

A one-time payment of €69 grants you lifetime access to all content on the platform. Your account is personal and may not be shared with others. Access is delivered immediately after payment via a PIN-based login system.
